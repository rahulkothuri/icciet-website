Step by Step

Camera Ready Paper Submission

Please ensure the following steps are completed for submitting the camera-ready paper and completing the registration.

1

Prepare Your Camera-Ready Manuscript

  • Revise your accepted paper according to the reviewers' comments.
  • The camera-ready paper must strictly follow the prescribed conference template. The template and page limit will be announced along with the publication partner.
  • Submit the camera-ready version on or before the camera-ready deadline (to be announced).
  • Papers must meet the plagiarism policy; the plagiarism check excludes references and biographies.
2

Author Registration

  • At least one author must register for each accepted paper.
  • Registration is required for presentation and inclusion in the proceedings.
  • The registration fee includes the certificate and proceedings.
  • The fee varies by track; detailed information is available via the registration desk.
  • Payment details will be sent in the acceptance letter.
3

Preparing & Uploading Camera-Ready Submission

Before creating your PDF: Proofread your source document thoroughly and ensure it conforms to the conference template.

  1. Finalise your manuscript in the prescribed template.
  2. Verify formatting: layout, page limit, figures, tables, and references.
  3. Generate a compliant PDF of your paper.
  4. Upload the PDF via the conference submission portal (link to be announced).
4

Presentation

  • All registered authors present a 15-minute PowerPoint presentation in hybrid mode.
  • Presentations consist of a 12-minute talk followed by 3 minutes of Q&A.
  • Prepare a concise PowerPoint to fit the time.
  • Limited online presentations may be allowed for valid reasons, upon permission.
5

Registration

At least one author must register.
