Scope of the Conference

The conference focuses on mutually sharing the advances and innovative technologies for the scientists, scholars, engineers and students from different universities and industry practitioners, to present ongoing research activities in the recent trends of Computer Science and Engineering. This conference addresses the relevant topics and research issues in the vicinity of Computational Intelligence and hence to foster collaborations among stakeholders and researchers from distinct universities, national laboratories, government funding bodies and the industry.

Submission Guidelines

Submitted papers should not have been previously published nor be currently under consideration for publication elsewhere. All submissions will be thoroughly peer-reviewed by experts based on originality, significance, and clarity. Only papers presenting novel research results or successful innovative applications will be considered for publication in the conference proceedings.

Submission Policy

  • Only original research papers are accepted.
  • Review papers and short notes are not eligible.
  • Papers must follow the conference's prescribed format.
  • Non-compliant papers will not be considered for review.
  • Multiple submissions are strictly prohibited.
  • Papers must not be published or submitted elsewhere.

Plagiarism Policy

  • Papers with less than 13% plagiarism pass to review.
  • 21–30% plagiarism: returned for reduction and resubmission.
  • 31%+ plagiarism: rejected outright.
  • Plagiarism check excludes references and biographies.

Review Policy

  • Papers undergo single-blind review by at least two independent experts.
  • Reports are submitted within the review timeline.
  • The TPC oversees the process; reviewers receive certificates.
  • Reviewers must be research scholars or PhD holders.

Acceptance Policy

  • Papers must meet criteria for quality, novelty, research objectives, language, presentation, validation, analysis, and references.
  • Acceptance is communicated via the conference submission system.
  • Minor revisions may be requested before final acceptance.
  • Authors of rejected papers are notified immediately.
  • The final decision is made by the track's dedicated committee.

Selection Criteria

  • Relevance to the conference theme.
  • Originality & Innovation in research or application.
  • Academic Rigor: sound objectives, methodology, and findings.
  • Clarity & Structure in writing and organization.
  • Impact & Contribution to the field or ongoing advances.

Rejection Criteria

  • Non-alignment with conference themes.
  • Plagiarism or ethical violations.
  • Lack of originality or recycled work without new contributions.
  • Poor quality, insufficient detail, or weak methodology.
  • Failure to meet submission/formatting guidelines.

Malpractice Statement

ICCIET upholds the highest standards of academic integrity. Plagiarism, data falsification, redundant publication, or unethical behavior are strictly prohibited. All parties must adhere to ethical guidelines and maintain transparency, originality, and honesty. Suspected malpractice will be investigated and may lead to exclusion or retraction.
